About Sidhiti

Devotion, Delivered With Care

Sidhiti was born from a simple frustration - running from shop to shop the night before a festival, hoping we hadn't forgotten a single leaf or flower needed for the pooja. We decided no devotee should have to compromise on tradition due to lack of time.

Today we hand-curate every pooja kit ourselves - sourcing fresh patras, pure idols, quality dhoop, and every last item mentioned in the shastras - and pack them as one complete kit, so you can focus on the prayer, not the preparation.

  • 📜 Kits packed as per shastra-prescribed lists
  • 🚚 Doorstep delivery, timed for your festival
